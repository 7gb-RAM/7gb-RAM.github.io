**Role:** Team lead, team of three · **Program:** MIT DesignX incubator · **Year:** 2026

BuyBye is a live peer-to-peer marketplace for campus housing, launched in partnership with MIT Housing and deployed to more than 5,000 MIT students in its first week.

I led a team of three building the administrative platform behind the live marketplace:

- Built role-based access control for the platform.
- Built an operations analytics dashboard.
- Stack: Next.js, Supabase and PostgreSQL.
