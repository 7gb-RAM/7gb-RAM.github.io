**Lab:** Software Systems Lab, Montclair State University · **PI:** Dr. Vaibhav Anu

MAUQ-CLIP is a black-box uncertainty quantification framework for clinical large language model predictions. It is the first to treat missing evidence in a patient record as an uncertainty signal. On SEER uterine cancer records it raised accuracy from 94.0% to 98.5% and cut calibration error by 88%.

Published at IEEE HealthCom 2026. See [publications](/publications/).
